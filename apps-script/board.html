Standing

Available days
Days you have been in the pool so far, less leave, scaled by your working fraction.
Carried
Points you have actually earned, for the post you really worked.
Due
Your share of everything handed out so far, in proportion to your available days.
Per day
Carried ÷ available days. Compare it with the target rate above — everyone should sit near it.
Balance
Carried − Due, in points. One weekday third call is worth 1; a first call on Christmas, 13.2.

Negative means you are owed duty and are nearer the front of the queue. Positive means you have carried more than your share.

Balances are a redistribution, so across everyone they add up to zero.
